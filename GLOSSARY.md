# Portfolio

The personal site of Igor Borisenko, a senior full-stack developer. It presents his work to recruiters and clients and gives them a way to get in touch.

## Language

**Project**:
A product Igor worked on, presented by what was achieved rather than by what the product is called. A Project never shows its product name in text.
_Avoid_: Case, app, client work

**Outcome headline**:
The title of a Project: one sentence stating the result of the work. It stands in for the product name.
_Avoid_: Project name, project title

**Category tag**:
A short label naming the kind of product a Project is, such as "B2C platform" or "B2B marketplace".
_Avoid_: Type, label

**Case study**:
The long-form account of one Project: the challenge, Igor's role, the solution and the results.
_Avoid_: Project overview, project details

**Experience entry**:
One period of Igor's working history, titled by the kind of product and never by a product name. An entry for a plain employer that is not one of Igor's Projects keeps the employer's real name.
_Avoid_: Job, position, employer entry

**Résumé**:
The downloadable document of Igor's working history. It is the only place where product names appear.
_Avoid_: CV

**Portrait**:
The photograph of Igor shown in the hero.
_Avoid_: Avatar, headshot, face

**Wordmark**:
The site's brand as written text: the domain `igorborisenko.dev`.
_Avoid_: Logo, site name
